meditick — pill timer & reminder

privacy policy

effective august 4, 2026 · tinkersmithstudio

The short version: your medications, schedules and dose history stay on your phone. MediTick has no accounts, analytics, ads or cloud database. We cannot read your medication list. If you enable iCloud, meal times may sync through your personal iCloud account. Purchase and entitlement information is handled by Apple and RevenueCat as described below.

1. what we collect

MediTick does not collect or receive the medications, schedules, dose history, notes, streaks or settings you enter. This information is stored locally on your device. We operate no server that receives this health information.

If you purchase MediTick Pro, an anonymous app-user identifier and limited purchase and entitlement information are processed by RevenueCat as described in section 4.

2. what stays on your device

  • Medication and prescription details you enter
  • Dose logs (taken / skipped / snoozed) and adherence history
  • Reminder preferences and appearance settings
  • Supply counts and refill thresholds

3. optional icloud sync

If your device has iCloud enabled for MediTick, your meal times only may sync across your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This information is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Tinkersmithstudio does not receive or have access to it.

4. subscriptions (revenuecat)

MediTick Pro purchases are processed by Apple. To determine whether Pro access is active and to restore purchases, MediTick uses RevenueCat, our only third-party SDK. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and limited Apple transaction and entitlement information, such as the product identifier, purchase date and renewal state. This information is used only to manage and restore MediTick Pro access. RevenueCat does not receive your medications, schedules, dose history, notes or other locally stored health information.

RevenueCat processes this information according to its Privacy Policy. Any third-party service provider that processes information for MediTick is required to provide the same or an equivalent level of protection described in this policy.

5. notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification content, including medication names, is composed on-device and shown only on your screen. You can disable notifications for MediTick or hide notification previews at any time in iOS Settings.

6. retention, deletion and your choices

  • Export — back up your locally stored history as a file at any time.
  • Import — restore a previously exported file on a device you control.
  • Erase all — delete your locally stored MediTick data with one confirmed action.
  • Delete the app — deleting MediTick removes its locally stored data from that device.
  • Notifications — withdraw notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • iCloud — disable MediTick's iCloud access through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.

Locally stored information is retained until you erase it or delete the app. Meal-time information synced through iCloud is retained and controlled through your personal iCloud account. RevenueCat retains purchase and entitlement information according to its privacy policy and as necessary to provide purchase restoration and comply with applicable legal obligations.

To request deletion of information associated with MediTick or to ask a privacy question, email tinkersmithstudio@gmail.com. Because MediTick has no user accounts, we may need the anonymous app-user identifier associated with your installation to locate a RevenueCat record. We will delete information we control and, where applicable, assist with a request involving RevenueCat. Apple may retain transaction records under its own legal obligations and privacy policy.

7. children

MediTick is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect medication or other health information from children.

8. changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be identified in the app's release notes where appropriate.

9. contact

Questions or privacy requests? tinkersmithstudio@gmail.com
